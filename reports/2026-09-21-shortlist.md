## Notes

RDAP status is point-in-time and can change at any moment. "AVAILABLE" means the registry RDAP endpoint returned a not-found result during this scan; registration at a registrar remains the final check.

Trademark clearance is not complete legal clearance. Before adopting a parent-company brand, run jurisdiction-specific trademark searches for the intended Nice classes.
