# BrandForge Scoring Model v0.2

## Goal

Evaluate long-term technology brand assets instead of only checking domain availability.

## Score

Total: 100 points

- Brand uniqueness: 25
- Future technology fit: 25
- Pronunciation and memorability: 15
- Search pollution risk: 15
- Domain quality: 10
- Developer ecosystem fit: 10

## Strategic modifiers

For the user's target areas:

- AI infrastructure: +20
- Industrial software: +15
- Education technology: +10
- Quantitative finance: +10
- Game technology: +5

## Output

Rank candidates and generate a review report.
